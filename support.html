UCJC Apostolic App Support

About the App

The UCJC Apostolic App helps attendees stay connected during events through schedules, announcements, reminders, maps, messaging, and attendee resources.

Support & Contact

Frequently Asked Questions

I can't log in

Verify your email address and use the password reset option if necessary.

Notifications are not working

Check that notifications are enabled for the app in your device settings.

The schedule is not updating

Refresh the schedule and ensure your device has internet access.

Messages are not sending

Verify your internet connection and try again.

Troubleshooting

  1. Close and reopen the app.
  2. Check your internet connection.
  3. Update to the latest version.
  4. Restart your device.
