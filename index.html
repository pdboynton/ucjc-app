About
UCJC Holy Convocation
Version 1.0 · Progressive Web App
Built for the United Church of Jesus Christ to provide a seamless, spirit-filled conference experience. Features include a live schedule from Google Calendar, personalized bookmarks, push reminders, attendee directory, group and private chat, session notes, and session feedback.
